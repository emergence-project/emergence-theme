## Brief

Create a quiet reading structure that remains legible over both visual
environments.

## Decisions

- Keep the content column comfortably narrow.
- Use type and spacing before adding another container.
- Preserve visible focus and reduced-motion behavior.

## Result

The same hierarchy works from a phone-sized viewport to a wide desktop while
the background remains present but secondary.
