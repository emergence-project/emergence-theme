## Observation

Decoration is useful when it explains structure. When every section competes
for attention, hierarchy becomes harder to read.

## Why It Matters

Quiet separators, consistent spacing, and one clear accent make page state
visible without adding another visual layer.

## Applied here

Emergence lets the environment carry atmosphere while the interface stays
calm and predictable.
