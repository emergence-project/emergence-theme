## Observation

An image can establish place before any copy is read. It should not reduce the
contrast, stability, or navigation clarity of the content placed above it.

## Guardrails

- Preserve readable foreground surfaces.
- Keep crop behavior stable as the viewport changes.
- Use glow only where it communicates focus or state.

## Applied here

The Glacier and Galaxy environments share one structural grammar while using
different optical treatments.
